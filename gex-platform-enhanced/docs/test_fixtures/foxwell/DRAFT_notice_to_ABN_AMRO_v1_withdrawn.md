On 18 November 2026 HamburgOne shared with you, read-only on GreenEarthX, the returns case

Basis. It priced a generic methanol-synthesis train that bought hydrogen at a model default instead of
including our 120 MW electrolyser, applied a location for the Netherlands rather than Germany, ignored our
stated 10 % contingency, and relied on engineering defaults that were not listed as assumptions. Its
figures (project IRR −10.0 %, fixed capital €175.3M) are therefore not a fair statement of the project.

- The calculation has been corrected to price the saved Cost Basis line by line, with every remaining
  default listed.

  resolve before anything is published.
- An independent engineer, instructed by you (reference GEX-TEST-IE-NSEM-001), will review it.
- Only after that review will we publish a new version, with a reconciliation against version 1, and
  share it with you.
